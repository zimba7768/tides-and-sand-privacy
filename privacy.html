Tides and Sand Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Tides and Sand is a sandcastle game developed by Seabell. This page explains what information the game uses and how it is used.

Data collection

Tides and Sand does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no ad tracking, and no account sign-in required to play.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your level progress and stars, your settings, your Sea Glass balance, and the cosmetics you own and have equipped. To make sure a purchase is never delivered twice, it also keeps a one-way fingerprint of each purchase. All of this stays only on your device. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and uninstalling the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game uses vibration for haptic feedback, for example when waves crash. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings. The internet and network state permissions are used only by Google Play Billing, to show Sea Glass pack prices and process purchases. The game itself plays offline. It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, or any other device data.

Purchases

Tides and Sand offers optional in-app purchases of Sea Glass, the game's currency for cosmetic items, and a one time Starter Pack. Purchases are handled entirely through Google Play Billing. Tides and Sand does not see or store your payment information. Google's own privacy policy governs those transactions: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Tides and Sand does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
